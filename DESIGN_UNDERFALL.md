# The Underfall: combat, hunting ground, home, rival (Fable 5.2 design spec, condensed)

**Loop.** A *run* is one trip from your matchbox into the unlit Sump Ward and back. You hunt a **Fall** (things the
giant loses down the back of the shelves), fight spiders and the Duke's Tallymen for it, and carry out only what
your pack holds. Banked loot buys home tiers that make the next run safer.

**Weapons** (workbench)
- **Pick:** a toothpick spear. Recipe: 2 scrap + 1 fabric. Jab, jab, lunge for 12 / 12 / 24 damage, reach 1.9.
- **Snapband:** a hairpin fork with elastic. Recipe: 1 metal + 2 fabric. Hold to draw (0.8 s), release to fire:
  8→22 damage. Ammo is **grit**: 1 scrap makes 6.

**Enemies**
- **Dust Hopper** (jumping spider): HP 30, lunge 12. Cycles lurk → stalk → rear (telegraph) → lunge → recover
  (takes +50% damage). Drops silk, and a fang 60% of the time.
- **Cellar Weaver:** HP 80, web spit, bite 18.
- **Duke Tallow, Holder of the Fall Franchise:** a rival power under an Authority licence. Calloway sold him the
  right to everything that falls outside the Commons. He never fights. He's right about safety and wages and
  wrong about ownership.
- **Tallymen:** his wage crews (HP 45, hook 10 with a 0.45 s wind-up). They race you to drops and haul them to
  the cart. Never killed: at 0 HP they sit down for 20 s and drop their load.

**The Sump Ward:** tier 0, west of the Spill, through a gate. In order: Gate (claims table) → Wet Shelf → Drop
Field (cover: a domino, a die, a coin, a marble; Hoppers) → Webworks → the Cart. Every 150–210 s there is a
**Fall**: crumbs, wrapper foil, a pin spill, or rarely a button cell or a stray sweet. Collapse in the Sump and
half your haul goes to the cart, where you can steal it back.

**Home tiers**

| Tier | Cost | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Stash Tin | 4 scrap, 2 metal | Storage that survives a collapse |
| 2 Tealight Stove | 3 metal, 2 foil, 1 electronics | Hot Bowl: 2 food + 1 water → hunger +60, double regen 120 s |
| 3 Drip Barrel | 2 scrap, 2 fabric, 3 silk | Free water at home |
| 4 Fang Wall | 5 fangs, 2 foil | Each mounted fang +2 max HP (to 120) |
| 5 Pin Latch & Bell | 4 metal, 3 silk, 2 fangs | Stops raids |

**Ties to Chapter 1.** Community path: Calloway sold your levy to the Duke, so Tallymen are hostile until it's
paid. Authority path: they stay neutral unless provoked.

Duke Tallow:
- "Calloway taxes you for arriving. I only tax what you carry out. Tell me which of us is the thief."
- "Before my paper, eleven went into the dark in one dry season. Count my crews tonight. Then complain about the third."
- "Your levy was a bad debt. I bought it for nine tokens. You'll find I collect better than I pay."

**Build order:** combat kit → Sump and Hoppers → Fall and cart → home tiers 1–3 → Duke and Tallymen →
(cut first) raids, tiers 4–5, the Weaver, daily claims.
